TITLE: Novel Single-Cell RNA Biomarkers for Early Detection of Pancreatic Ductal Adenocarcinoma
AUTHOR: Dr. Alice Nakamoto, Institute for Advanced Oncology (2026)
COPYRIGHT: (C) 2026 Dr. Alice Nakamoto. All rights reserved. Commercial training prohibited without on-chain license.
ABSTRACT:
We identify a distinct 14-gene transcriptomic signature within CAFs (cancer-associated fibroblasts)
that precedes radiographic tumor detection by an average of 4.2 months. Expression profiling across
240 patient cohorts demonstrates sensitivity of 94.6% and specificity of 98.1%.
